About

Photo Assimilator Uploader discovers photos and videos in device folders selected by the user, such as the Camera folder, and sends chosen or new media directly to the Photo Assimilator server configured by that user.

The current AU-03 Internal testing build provides manual foreground Sync now while the transfer and permission model is validated. Background Wi-Fi Auto Upload is under development and is not enabled in the current test build.

The uploader contains no ads and no developer-operated analytics service in the current build.
