About

Photo Assimilator Uploader is the privacy-first mobile companion for a self-hosted Photo Assimilator library. It connects directly to your own server over a secure local network, VPN, or HTTPS—with no third-party cloud intermediaries, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking analytics.

Key Capabilities

  • Device Private Vault: Photos, videos, and private people profiles stay strictly on your personal device by default.
  • Selective Family Sharing: Group media into private albums and selectively share only designated albums with your household Photo Assimilator server.
  • On-Device Face Processing: Recognizes faces and bundles private name tags as advisory hints to family members during shared album uploads without sending raw biometric models to developer cloud services.
  • Automatic Background & Manual Sync: Automatic background Wi-Fi upload via Android WorkManager, resumable transfers for large video files, and on-demand foreground Sync now.
  • Offline Local Gallery: Browse high-resolution device originals even when offline or disconnected from your server.
  • Duplicate Prevention: SHA-256 preflight hashing prevents redundant re-transmissions.
