About

Photo Assimilator Uploader discovers photos and videos in user-selected device folders (such as the Camera folder) and backs them up directly to your self-hosted Photo Assimilator server address.

Current Internal testing builds (v0.5.1) support both manual Sync now and automatic background Wi-Fi synchronization via Android WorkManager, resumable transfer after network interruptions, duplicate prevention with SHA-256 content hashes, an offline local gallery browser, and on-demand folder archive upload.

The uploader contains no ads, no third-party trackers, and routes no media through developer cloud services.
