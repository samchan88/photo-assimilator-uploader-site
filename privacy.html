Privacy Policy

Photo Assimilator Uploader · Last updated 7 November 2026

About Privacy Policy Support

Photo Assimilator Uploader is an Android companion for a self-hosted, user-controlled Photo Assimilator photo and video library. It respects user privacy by keeping device media, private albums, and personal face tags local by default, sending media directly to the user-configured Photo Assimilator server only when explicitly synchronized or shared.

Information the app accesses

  • Photos and videos: Media files stored on your Android device through MediaStore when permission is granted.
  • Media location metadata: When permission is granted (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION), original capture GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) are read to preserve location metadata during transfer.
  • Media metadata: Filename, MIME type, size, timestamps, MediaStore identifier/URI, and relative folder path.
  • Content hashes: A SHA-256 hash calculated locally on the device to prevent uploading redundant duplicate bytes.
  • Device Private Vault: Private albums, media associations, and private people names stored strictly on-device in sandboxed application database storage.
  • On-device face hints: Facial detection and embedding analysis run on-device using local models. When you choose to share a private album with your family, bounding boxes and user-assigned names may be attached as advisory hints so household members do not need to re-tag faces. Raw facial biometric vectors are never sent to developer cloud services.
  • Server connection settings: The user-configured Photo Assimilator service address and a dedicated, revocable bearer device token.
  • Local sync state: Selected folder rules, baseline IDs, queue status, and server-confirmed resumable byte offsets.

The app does not read contacts, text messages, call logs, phone state, or device location outside photo EXIF data.

How photos and videos are used

Media access is the app's core function. The app allows users to:

  • Browse local device photos and videos in an offline gallery without connecting to any server.
  • Create Device Private Vault albums that remain strictly on the phone.
  • Selectively share specific albums with your household Photo Assimilator server.
  • Automatically back up selected folders (e.g., DCIM/Camera) over unmetered Wi-Fi.

The app does not modify or delete original photos or videos from your phone after upload.

Where media is sent

Media files, album manifests, and transfer metadata are sent strictly and directly to the Photo Assimilator server address configured by the user. The app does not route photos, videos, or face data through any developer-operated cloud servers.

If you unshare a shared album, an unshare notification is sent to your self-hosted server, which safely moves media no longer referenced by other shared albums to the server's 30-day Recycle Bin.

If an unencrypted http:// address is configured, network traffic is visible on the local network. A private network (e.g. Tailscale or WireGuard) or HTTPS is strongly recommended.

Device credential security

The uploader uses a dedicated, revocable Auto Upload device token rather than the Photo Assimilator Owner master password. The token is stored securely using Android Keystore encryption.

Local storage and retention

All local database records (private albums, private person profiles, and sync queues) are stored inside Android's sandboxed private storage. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device private album and profile records. Uploaded items already present on your self-hosted server are preserved according to your server retention policies.

Sharing, advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party tracking services, and no developer-operated analytics. The developer does not collect, sell, or monetize your photos, videos, or usage data.

Google Play may process diagnostic telemetry associated with downloading, installing, and updating the application under Google's standard terms and privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Photo Assimilator Uploader is a self-hosted personal library companion and is not directed to children.

User choices and controls

  • Grant, deny, or revoke Android photo, video, and media location permissions at any time.
  • Keep media strictly local in the Device Private Vault or choose which albums to share.
  • Enable or disable automatic background Wi-Fi synchronization.
  • Unshare any previously shared album, moving server media to the server Recycle Bin.
  • Revoke the device token directly from the Photo Assimilator server.

Changes to this policy

This policy is updated whenever changes to app functionality affect how data is accessed, stored, or transferred.
