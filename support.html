Common Troubleshooting Tips

  • Google Play Download Stalling: If downloading from Google Play Internal Testing stalls on devices with VPN or Tailscale active, temporarily disconnect Tailscale while initiating the Play Store download. Reconnect once the app is installed.
  • Connection Not Reachable: Ensure your phone can reach the Photo Assimilator server IP/port (port 8100 on Home Assistant). If using Tailscale, ensure Tailscale is connected on both device and server.
  • Samsung Power Saving: For reliable background synchronization when the phone screen is off, set app Battery to Unrestricted and disable Samsung aggressive power saving for the companion app.
